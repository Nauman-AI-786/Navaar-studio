About Navaar Studio

Made for poetry, reels and everyday creators.

Navaar Studio began with a simple idea: Urdu text should look beautiful on screen, without fighting broken fonts or complicated software. It supports Nastaliq and right-to-left writing properly, works on a phone, and keeps your work private because everything runs in your own browser.
