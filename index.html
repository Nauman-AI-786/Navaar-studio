Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first scene.

What kind of content can I create?
Short poetry, quotes, reflections and announcement-style videos in a 9:16 format, built from a single line of Urdu or English text.
Can I control Urdu typography?
Yes — switch between a classic nastaliq-style look and a cleaner modern face using the Typography control in the studio above.
Can I add audio?
You can choose a voice or music direction to set the tone, shown as a label on the preview and export. This demo page does not include licensed audio files.
What export quality is shown here?
The Export HD preview button records the animated on-screen composition at 540×960 and downloads it as a short .webm video you can open or share.
How is my creative work handled?
Everything you type and save stays in your own browser's local storage on this device — nothing is uploaded to a server from this page.
Can I return to saved projects?
Yes — use Save project to add the current line to Saved projects below, then Load it back into the studio at any time.
