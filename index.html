Welcome to Navaar Studio

Give your Urdu words a cinematic life.

Navaar Studio is a free online video maker for Urdu and English poetry, shayari, quotes, lyrics, reels, shorts and promos. Everything runs in your browser: no install, no design skills needed.

What can you do here?

Video EditorTimeline, trim, split, text, shapes, filters, speed, volume, voiceover.
Quick Poetry VideoOne line, your mood, font and background, then export.
Photo + Lyrics VideoPhotos, lyrics and your own audio in a short video.
TemplatesGhalib, Mir, Iqbal and more, plus quotes and empty styles.
DownloadSave videos and PNG frames to your device.
Free AccountSign up to save projects and use them anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Pick a template or type your own verse.
  2. Choose font, background, photos, layout and audio.
  3. Export and download, then share anywhere.

Who is it for?

Poets and shayari pages, content creators, students, small businesses and anyone who wants quick, beautiful vertical videos in Urdu or English.
