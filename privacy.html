Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Navaar Studio ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: if you create an account or sign in with email, Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile identifier from the sign-in provider.
  • Feedback: the name, optional email, feedback type, star rating and message you submit through the feedback form.
  • Projects: the text, style choices and settings of projects you save while signed in.
  • Payments: if you buy a plan, payment is handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy. We do not see or store your card details; we only receive your plan and subscription status.
  • Files you add: photos and audio you use in the video maker are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To save your projects and manage your subscription.
  • To review and improve the service using the feedback you send.
  • To prevent spam and abuse.

3. Public feedback

Your name, star rating, feedback type and message may be shown publicly in the "What people are saying" section of the website. Your email address is never shown publicly. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in your feedback message.

4. Sign in with Google and Facebook

When you use Google or Facebook to sign in, we only receive basic profile information (name, email and profile identifier). We do not post on your behalf and we do not access your friends list, contacts or other data.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers to run the website:

  • Supabase – database and sign-in.
  • Railway – server hosting.
  • Lemon Squeezy – payments.
  • Google and Meta (Facebook) – only if you choose to sign in with them.

6. Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to run the service. We use industry-standard measures to protect it, but no online service can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Delete your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all data linked to it (account, saved projects and feedback) at any time.

To request deletion, use the Feedback form on our website, choose the type "General feedback", write "Delete my data" and include the email address or the Facebook/Google account name you signed in with. We will remove your data within 30 days.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Navaar Studio from your Facebook settings under "Apps and Websites".

8. Children

Navaar Studio is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, please contact us through the Feedback form on our website.
